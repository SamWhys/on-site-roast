Room to grow ✨
🔴 High impact
Visibility of system status is weak

Form submissions don't confirm success or failure clearly. Users click "Sign up" and are left wondering if anything happened — a common cause of duplicate submissions and drop-off.

Nielsen Heuristic #1 · Visibility of system status
🔴 High impact
Headline tells, doesn't show

"All-in-one platform for teams" is feature-led and vague. Visitors don't know what problem you solve in the first 3 seconds. Try leading with the outcome users care about.

C.L.E.A.R. Framework · Concise + Relevant
🟡 Medium impact
CTA labels are generic

"Get started," "Learn more," and "Submit" appear 14 times across the page. Specific verbs ("Start my free trial," "See pricing") set expectations and lift click-through.

UX writing · Action-led microcopy
🟡 Medium impact
Color contrast fails WCAG AA

Light gray body text on white (#A8A8A8 on #FFF) falls below the 4.5:1 contrast ratio needed for accessibility. Users with low vision and outdoor mobile users will struggle.

WCAG 2.1 · Accessibility
🟢 Low effort win
Form has 8 fields. Could be 3.

Phone number, company size, and job title are all marked required but aren't used until later. Each extra field drops conversion ~5%. Move them to onboarding.

Form design · Progressive disclosure
🟢 Low effort win
Strong social proof, hidden away

Testimonials and customer logos sit at the bottom of the page. Move at least one trust signal above the fold to reduce friction on first impression.
